Privacy Policy for BlockFit

Last updated: February 15, 2026

Introduction

BlockFit ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

BlockFit collects minimal information:

  • Game Data: High scores, game settings, and progress are stored locally on your device only.
  • Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob for advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for personalized ads.
  • Google Play Games Services: If you sign in with Google Play Games, your display name and profile photo may be visible on leaderboards. This data is managed by Google.
  • In-App Purchases: Purchase transactions are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information such as credit card numbers. We only store your purchase status (e.g., whether ads have been removed) locally on your device.
  • Analytics: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the game experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the game
  • To display relevant advertisements
  • To process in-app purchases and unlock purchased features
  • To display leaderboards and achievements via Google Play Games Services
  • To improve user experience

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Services: For leaderboards and achievements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: For processing in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy

In-App Purchases

BlockFit offers optional in-app purchases to enhance your experience. All payment processing is handled securely by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment details. Purchased items and their status are stored locally on your device.

Data Storage

All game progress, settings, and purchase status are stored locally on your device. We do not store any personal data on external servers.

Children's Privacy

BlockFit does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game is suitable for all ages.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Reset game data by clearing app data or reinstalling
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  • Sign out of Google Play Games Services at any time
  • Restore your in-app purchases on any device using the same Google account

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app or on this page.
